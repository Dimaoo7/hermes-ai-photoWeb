Questions

Frequently asked

When is the best time to book a date?

For high season (May–September), 3–6 months ahead. If your date is already set, write as early as you can: I confirm within a day.

Do you work outside Montenegro?

Yes, I travel across the Balkans and Europe. Travel and accommodation are counted separately for shoots longer than one day.

How long until the photographs are ready?

Weddings take 3–4 weeks; portraits and love stories 7–10 days. I send the first 5–10 frames the next day.

What if it rains?

There is always a plan B: awnings, old towns, cafés and mood. Rain is not an obstacle — it is different light.

Can we simply talk before booking?

Of course. Call or write on WhatsApp — we will discuss the date, the location and everything that matters to you.
