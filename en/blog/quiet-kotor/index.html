Quiet Kotor

I want the same — write to me

A slow walk through the old town and frames where you recognise yourself.

Kotor is best alone: narrow streets, shade, stone. We walked without a route and stopped where the light pleased us. The result is portraits without catalogue poses — just people and their city.
