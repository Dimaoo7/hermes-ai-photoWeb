Perast, Montenegro — June 2026

Milena & Marko

I want the same — write to me

A quiet day by the water: white stone, sails and plenty of space between frames.

Milena and Marko wanted no staging and no crowds. We met early while Perast was still asleep, walked down to the water and talked about themselves. By evening — confetti and the first dance on the terrace. That is all I remember about that day.
